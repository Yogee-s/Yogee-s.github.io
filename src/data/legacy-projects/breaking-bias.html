Breaking Bias in LLMs

AI Safety Research · University of Toronto exchange (ECE324) · Jan – May 2025

Large Language ModelsPython PyTorchPrompt Injection
LLM Research

How biased and easily manipulated are LLMs when they are used to score written work, and can a committee of judges make them fairer? We attacked LLM judges with adversarial essays, then compared how single judges and committees scored the same inputs.

Approach

Attack
Write adversarial essays designed to mislead the judges.
Judge
Score each essay with single judges and judge committees.
Compare
Measure score shifts and disagreement to see which setup resists manipulation.

Key finding: text with disrupted meaning could still score highly when its wording looked fluent.
