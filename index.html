BoxBunny: Intelligent Boxing Robot (Capstone)

Software Lead | CDE4301 Innovation and Design Capstone | Aug 2025 - Present

Work in Progress

Building a semi-autonomous boxing coach that combines robot actuation, perception models, and AI feedback into one closed-loop training system.

  • Designing the ROS-based software architecture for perception, decision, control, and feedback modules.
  • Running CV and action-recognition models on Jetson to interpret punch execution and body positioning.
  • Fusing IMU punch dynamics with vision outputs to quantify technique quality and consistency.
  • Using LLMs to generate coach-like, human-readable feedback from multimodal training metrics.
  • Building adaptive logic to personalize drill difficulty while keeping training safer than frequent sparring.

Teleco Human Localization at Osaka Expo 2025

Research Internship, The University of Osaka | May 2025 - Aug 2025

Deployed

Built a software pipeline to localize people from a third-person camera into the robot SLAM frame for socially aware gaze and interaction at deployment scale.

  • Used the latest YOLO model (YOLOv12) for human keypoint and Teleco calibration detection.
  • Implemented camera-to-map coordinate transformation with Kabsch solver and MQTT integration.
  • Built pixel-to-height calibration for gaze control using Teleco as a reference object.
  • Achieved reported mapping errors mostly around 0.02m to 0.06m and height error under 10cm in most tests.
  • System deployed at the Yoshikawa Lab pavilion, Osaka Expo 2025.

A*STAR Internship: RL for Inspire Dexterous Hand

Research Internship, A*STAR ARTC | May 2024 - Aug 2024

Research

Adapted a dexterous manipulation RL pipeline from Adroit hand to Inspire hand, focusing on simulation compatibility, observation mapping, and reward-design stability.

  • Converted Inspire hand URDF to MuJoCo XML and added missing constraints, damping, and collision parameters.
  • Mapped demonstration observations from Adroit (39D) to Inspire (21D) for DAPG training.
  • Grouped joints for a 6-actuator Inspire setup and tuned training behavior under time constraints.
  • Investigated local maxima and reward-shaping issues, then simplified task/object complexity to improve outcomes.
