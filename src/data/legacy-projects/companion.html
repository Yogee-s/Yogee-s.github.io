01

It doesn't just listen · it reacts

The robot's mood is a direct response to yours.

Sees you

The camera finds your face and keeps it in view.

Reads your mood

It measures how positive and how energetic you look, and passes that to the AI.

Shows how it feels

Every reply carries a mood tag that picks one of 13 animated faces on its screen.

02

What happens in one conversation turn

You stop talking, and it starts replying in under 0.8 seconds, all on the robot.

The voice loop

  1. Hears you finish and cuts out the clip of your speech.
  2. Writes down what you said, as you say it.
  3. Thinks of a reply, with your mood and the chat so far in the prompt.
  4. Speaks it sentence by sentence, so it starts talking before the full reply is ready.

Meanwhile, the face

  • Expression switches to match the reply's mood tag.
  • Lips move in time with the voice, about 30 times a second.

It runs on a separate branch, so the face never holds up the voice.

03

The hardware · designed and built solo

A custom head that can nod and turn, and one computer wired to every part.

Mechanism Two motors, one gear, a head that nods and turns

Both motors drive a shared gear, so together they control both directions of movement.

Nod (tilt)

Both motors turn the same way.

Turn (pan)

The motors turn opposite ways.

Six versions to get it smooth

Designed in SolidWorks. Each version fixed a problem: cable routing, pivot alignment and 3D-print warping.
