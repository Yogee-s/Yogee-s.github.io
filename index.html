Software Lead · CDE4301 Capstone · Aug 2025 – Present

Human Localization for Teleco Robot — Osaka Expo 2025

Research Intern · University of Osaka · May – Aug 2025

YOLOv12 SLAM Kabsch Solver MQTT Python
Deployed

Camera-to-SLAM localization pipeline for socially aware robot gaze at Expo scale.

  • Hard problem: image-plane camera detections had to be projected into the SLAM map in the robot perspective/frame, despite view-angle changes, depth ambiguity, and detection noise
  • YOLOv12 keypoint detection + Kabsch camera-to-map transform
  • Pixel-to-height calibration for gaze control via Teleco reference
  • 0.02–0.06 m mapping error, <10 cm height error
  • Deployed at Yoshikawa Lab pavilion, Osaka Expo 2025
